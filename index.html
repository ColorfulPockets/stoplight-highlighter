🚦 Stoplight Highlighter

No file selected
How to use: Hover over a sentence, then press G for green, Y for yellow, or R for red. Press C to clear the highlight. Click anywhere inside a sentence to split it into two independently highlightable pieces at that point. To highlight a specific portion, select text and pick a colour from the popup. Click Export to save the annotated file. Exported files use [green]…[/green] / [yellow]…[/yellow] / [red]…[/red] markup and can be re-imported to continue editing.
